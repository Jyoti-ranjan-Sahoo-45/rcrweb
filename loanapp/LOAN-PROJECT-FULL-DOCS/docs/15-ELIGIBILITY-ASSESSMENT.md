# 15 — Eligibility and Internal Assessment

## Basic eligibility
Customer-facing basic eligibility should only determine whether the customer may continue, needs more information, or does not match basic product criteria.

It is not a final approval decision.

## Internal assessment
May include:
- applicant profile
- income/turnover
- existing obligations
- repayment capacity
- requested amount
- document result
- risk observations
- approved data from external checks if integrated
- recommended amount
- recommended tenure
- recommended pricing
- pre-disbursement conditions

## Rules
- assessment is staff-only
- customer never sees internal notes unless explicitly intended
- recommendation and final decision are separate records
- staff actions require permission
- assessment updates are auditable
