Last Updated: October 14, 2026 | Effective Date: October 14, 2026

Digital Goods Fulfillment Notice: All products offered on RCR Groups (including HTML5 website templates, UI kits, CSS stylesheets, and source code packages) are intangible digital goods. No physical parcel, CD, or hard copy is dispatched via postal courier.

1. Electronic Delivery Process

Delivery of our HTML templates is conducted electronically over high-speed secure servers:

  • Instant Automated Fulfillment: Immediately upon successful transaction confirmation from Cashfree, our automated fulfillment system generates an encrypted download package and sends an email to the address specified during checkout.
  • On-Screen Confirmation: Once Cashfree confirms payment completion, your browser will also display an on-screen order summary and instant download button.

2. Delivery Timelines

  • Ready-Made HTML Templates: Delivery is instantaneous (typically within 1 to 5 minutes of payment confirmation). If there is any server congestion or high email queuing, delivery will occur within a maximum of 2 hours.

Because all our products and source code archives are delivered digitally over the internet, Shipping & Handling Fees are 100% FREE (₹0.00) for all digital products across India and globally.

Your digital download package will arrive as a secure .ZIP archive containing:

  • All raw, unminified, and minified HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript source code files.
  • Icons, vector graphics (SVG), and responsive sample assets.
