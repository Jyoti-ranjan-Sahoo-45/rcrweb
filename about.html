About RCR Groups

Building high-performance digital foundations for modern businesses and creators.

Mission Statement: At RCR Groups, our mission is to make world-class, ultra-fast, and beautiful web experiences accessible to every entrepreneur, startup, and developer without expensive subscriptions or complicated bloatware.

Who We Are

RCR Groups is an Indian web engineering and digital template development studio headquartered in Gurugram, Haryana. We design and distribute production-grade HTML5 website templates, UI toolkits, and offer custom frontend engineering for businesses globally.

We believe in clean code, semantic HTML markup, pure responsive CSS3, and lightweight vanilla JavaScript. By eliminating heavy framework overhead, our websites deliver instant load speeds, optimal SEO search rankings, and rock-solid reliability across all devices.
